UniMatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026
Developer: UniMatch App
Contact: putopoli43@gmail.com

UniMatch is a campus dating app that helps university students meet people at their school. This policy explains what data we collect, why we use it, and your choices.

Who this applies to

UniMatch is intended for adults (18+) who use a university or other institutional email address to verify they are part of a campus community.

Data we collect

  • Account & verification: email address, one-time verification codes, and the university name derived from your email domain
  • Profile: display name, date of birth, gender, course, year, bio, interests, photos you upload, and preferences (e.g. who you want to see)
  • Matching & chat: likes/passes, matches, messages, and read status
  • Safety: reports, blocks, and unmatch actions you take
  • Device notifications: an Expo/Firebase push token so we can send match and message alerts on your device
  • Technical: basic app and server logs needed to run and secure the service (e.g. timestamps, error events)

We do not sell your personal data.

How we use data

  • Create and secure your account
  • Show you relevant people at your university
  • Enable matches, chat, and notifications
  • Enforce safety tools (block, report, unmatch) and our rules
  • Operate, debug, and improve the service

Legal bases (EEA/UK, including Portugal)

Where required, we process data to perform the contract with you (providing the app), with your consent where needed (e.g. notifications permission on the device), and for legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention, and improving reliability — balanced against your rights.

Sharing

We use service providers who process data on our behalf, for example:

  • Supabase — database, authentication-related storage, file storage for photos
  • Expo / Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivery of push notifications on Android
  • Resend (or similar) — sending verification emails

These providers only process what is needed to provide their service. We do not share your profile or messages with other users beyond what the app already shows (e.g. your public profile to people who can discover you; messages with your matches).

Photos & messages

Profile photos are stored so other eligible users can see them in Discover/matches. Messages are visible to the people in that match. Do not upload content you are not comfortable sharing in that context.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymise personal data that identifies you, except where we must keep limited records for security, legal, or abuse-prevention reasons (e.g. report history) for a reasonable period.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, and to object or complain to a supervisory authority (in Portugal: CNPD). Contact us at the email above to exercise these rights.

Children

UniMatch is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls, and row-level security on our database. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in the EU and/or other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses) offered by those providers.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, when changes are significant, provide a more prominent notice in the app or by email when possible.
